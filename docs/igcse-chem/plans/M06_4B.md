# 6.4 Making Salts: Titration Calculations — unit plan (IGCSE_CHEM / M06_4B)

Wolsey Hall Module 6, Topic Four (6.4), second half. Built from book spread **11.8
"Finding concentration by titration"** (pages 142–143), with one recap slide of 11.6
(titration as a method, taught in `M06_4A`) and the Chapter 11 Checkup (question 8) as the
guide to the hardest form. **Extended content throughout.** The track's second
**calculation** unit, built like `M05_2`: one method, the same order every time.
English-only (`bilingual: false`), so no `vn*` twins.

## Objective

The student can give the job of each piece of titration apparatus (volumetric pipette,
burette, conical flask, indicator, white tile); say what the **end-point** is; read a
burette at the bottom of the meniscus and find **volume used = final − initial**; explain
**concentration** in mol/dm³ and the **standard solution**; change cm³ into dm³ (÷ 1000);
use the calculation triangle (moles = concentration × volume in dm³) three ways; and work
**the four steps** — (1) moles of the solution you know, (2) the ratio from the equation,
(3) moles of the other solution, (4) its concentration = moles ÷ volume in dm³ — for an
acid, for an alkali ("the other way round"), and for a **volume** when both concentrations
are known. Explains why a **weak acid** still reacts completely.

## The mistakes the unit is built around

| Mistake | Where it is caught |
|---|---|
| Adding the burette readings, or using the final reading alone | deck c3, workbook f3, Source Analysis 1 |
| Reading the edge of the meniscus / from above | deck hotspot a2, Questions sq1 |
| Dividing by 100, or multiplying, when changing cm³ → dm³ | deck estimate a3, workbook p1, quiz 3 |
| Leaving a volume in cm³ in Step 1 or Step 4 (1000× out) | deck c9 and c14, workbook p3/p5/c3, quiz 4 and 6, Questions sq2 |
| Ignoring the ratio, or using it upside down | deck predict a5 and c8, workbook p4/c1/c3, quiz 5 and 10, Questions sq4 |
| "More acid was needed, so the acid is weaker" | deck predict a5 → c9, deck c10 |
| Dividing by the other solution's volume | workbook c1, quiz 10 |
| Starting Step 1 from the unknown / from the flask | deck c7 and c13, Questions sq5 |
| Forgetting ×1000 when a volume is asked for in cm³ | deck c12, workbook c2, quiz 9 |
| "A weak acid only reacts as far as its ions go" | deck c11, Source Analysis 2 |
