# U02_5 · 2.5 Constructing and Solving Equations (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U02_5`), built to the 2.3+ unit shape
and deck density in [../algebra-engines.md](../algebra-engines.md).

**Objective:** I can say what an equation is and what solve means, check an
answer by putting it back in, undo each step with its inverse operation — the
last step first — and turn "I think of a number…" into an equation and solve it.
**Key words:** equation, solve, unknown, inverse operation, check, flow chart —
the section's two margin words (inverse operation, solve), the word the deck adds
(equation), and the three the method leans on.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U02_5`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here.

## Cut / changed from the classroom deck
- **Both hand votes → `predict`** (slides 5, 17), settled on the next slide. The
  ANS_* vote cards (a raised hand by each answer) were dropped from diagrams.js.
- **Check It! (room game) → a `sort`** of eight of its cards; the widget is not ported.
- **Whiteboard slides → activities**: one question typed or reversed, the rest in
  a reveal (slides 10, 11, 14, 20). Slide 11's became a check, not an activity,
  to keep activities at 10.
- **Folded:** "Mr Bowen's Number" (question only) into "Call It n" (slide 13).
- **Starter:** the three boxes became a typed `algebra` solve; `20 − □ = 11` stays
  in a reveal because the engine refuses "a number minus the letter".
- **Homework slide** (find the mistakes) keeps the drawing and becomes a `sort` by
  slip; its reveal moved into the activity's explanation. A `hotspot` was
  considered and rejected: hotspots strip every `<text>`, so the lines vanish.
- **Angles** and **the plant** became checks (their reveals would have given
  the answer away); **age** keeps its reveal.
- **Cut:** the homework-assignment slide, the "Lesson Complete!" exit hero (the
  exit question is the checklist's check), the date, "7 written panels", and
  every whiteboard / pairs / hands instruction.
