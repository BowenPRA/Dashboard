## The drill (NUM_DRILL, long-div with remainders)
The bus-stop method again, but here the final remainder matters: a test says
"yes" only when the remainder is 0, so dividing and reading the remainder is the
honest check — especially for 7, which has no test. The Stretch rung includes
`3960 ÷ 7 = 565 r5`, proving 7 does not divide the hook number.
