# U04_4 · 4.4 Dividing decimals (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the **4.4 run of the classroom deck**
`content/y7-math/U04_1_4` (4.1–4.4 in one period: slides 14–17, the homework's
line d, slides 19, 20 and 22) and the **dividing half of** `U04_review` (÷ a 2-digit
number, the answer that never ends, the conjecture and the mean), built to the
number-engines unit shape in [../number-engines.md](../number-engines.md) and the
Unit 4 notes in [../decimal-engines.md](../decimal-engines.md).
**English and French only** (`meta.vietnamese: false`).

**Objective:** I can divide a decimal by a whole number the short way — the point goes
straight up, a 0 goes in where a digit is too small to divide, and zeros are added
after the point until the remainder is 0; divide by a 2-digit number by writing its
times table first; check every answer with the inverse calculation; and read "share
equally" as divide.
**Key words:** inverse calculation, estimation, share equally, each, conjecture, mean —
the check the section is built on, the book's two margin words (estimation,
conjecture), the two story words, and the mean that tests the conjecture.
**Classroom twins:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U04_1_4` ("Maths 4.1–4.4 · Decimals")
and `y7-math/U04_review` ("Maths 4 · Bigger Numbers, Real Problems"). The classroom
`meta.dashboard` back-links are not set by this build — U04_1_4 holds one pointer and
feeds four units.
