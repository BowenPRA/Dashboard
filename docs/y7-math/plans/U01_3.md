# U01_3 · 1.3 Lowest Common Multiples (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U01_3`), built to the template 1.2
established (ADAPTATION-PLAN §8).

**Objective:** I can find the lowest common multiple of two numbers by listing
their multiples, and I know the English words for it.
**Key words:** multiple, common, common multiple, lowest common multiple, factor
— the one that costs marks is **common** (everyday English says *ordinary*;
maths says *shared*).

## The drill (NUM_DRILL, times-sprint)
A timed grid of single facts (`NumberDrill.jsx` times-sprint view). Multiples are
just times tables, and listing them is the LCM method, so the drill sharpens the
4×, 6× and mixed tables against a countdown. Score is the share of facts right.

## Assessment coverage (6 items, distractors = diagnoses)
1 multiple · 2 common (both key-words-in-context) · 3 LCM of 4 and 6 · 4 where one
number divides the other (`LCM(3,6)=6`) · 5 LCM from a list (`8,12→24`, multiply
trap) · 6 word problem (two lighthouses). No item copies a check or workbook
question; maths is in `$$…$$`.
