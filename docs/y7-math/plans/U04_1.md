# U04_1 · 4.1 Ordering decimals (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the **first five slides** of the classroom
deck (`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U04_1_4` — 4.1 to 4.4 taught in
ONE period), plus slide 18a, built out to a full unit from Cambridge Lower
Secondary Mathematics 7 (2nd ed.) section 4.1, to the number-engines unit shape in
[../number-engines.md](../number-engines.md). Engine and schemas:
[../decimal-engines.md](../decimal-engines.md) §4.1.

**English and French only** — `meta.vietnamese: false`; every English string has
a `…Fr` twin and none a `…Vn` one; screens toggle EN → FR.

**Objective:** I can say a decimal the English way ("three point two five"),
compare two decimals — whole-number part first, then fill with zeros — use < and
>, and put a list in order of size starting with the smallest or the largest,
even when the question says fastest, cheapest or heaviest.
**Key words:** compare, whole-number part, decimal part, less than, greater than,
in order of size — the section's verb, the two parts every comparison starts
from, the two signs in words, and the book's instruction.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U04_1_4` (one deck, four units:
U04_2–U04_4 carry the same entry). The classroom lesson's `meta.dashboard`
back-link is the integrator's to set — it holds one `{ track, unit }`, and this
unit is the first of the four.
