# U04_3 · 4.3 Multiplying decimals (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the **multiplying part** of the classroom
speed-run deck (`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U04_1_4` — slides 10–13,
17, 18c and 21) and of the 2-digit, money and word-problem work of the optional
review day (`content/y7-math/U04_review` — slides 3–5, 9–10, 14), built to the
number-engines unit shape in [../number-engines.md](../number-engines.md). The
engine, its schema and its slips are in [../decimal-engines.md](../decimal-engines.md)
§4.3. **English and French only** (`meta.vietnamese: false`).

**Objective:** I can multiply a decimal by a whole number: estimate first, take the
point out and multiply, put the point back with the same number of decimal places,
and check the answer against the estimate; use one fact to find others; write money
to 2 decimal places; and solve word problems that multiply.
**Key words:** estimate, product, decimal point, whole number, each, exact — the
method's word, the answer's name, the thing that moves, the other number, the word
that turns a story into ×, and the word that lets zeros go.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U04_1_4` ("Maths 4.1–4.4 · Decimals")
and `y7-math/U04_review` ("Maths 4 · Bigger Numbers, Real Problems"). The classroom
decks' `meta.dashboard` back-links are not set by this build (the lessons repo is
read-only to it; U04_1_4 is shared by 4.1–4.4).

## ADDED — the `pointback` activity and the tap row
The classroom deck says "take the point out, put it back" and has the class write
it on whiteboards. The self-study deck makes the point a thing you place: the digits
of the whole-number product are tiles with a gap between every two, and two faded
0 tiles always stand in front (so 0.03 × 3 → 0.09 can be made without the row giving
the zero away). The same row is the task's *point back* stage. A wrong tap is named:
decimals make it smaller, counted from the left, a lost placeholder zero, a place too
many or too few — each with the estimate beside it.
