The idea is "there and back", taught twice. The five checks: which is 5²
(distractor 10), √144, cube-vs-square (√64 vs the cube root), trapping √45 between
two integers, and consecutive square numbers. Correct answers spread B/A/B/A/C.

## The drill (NUM_DRILL, long-mult, squaring)
The two-digit column-multiplication grid again, but each problem is a number
times itself — `13²`, `25²`, `47²`, `99²` by hand. The square list stops being
magic once you can build it. Every value is derived by `NumberDrill.jsx`.

## Assessment coverage (6 items, distractors = diagnoses)
1 a square (`7²`) · 2 a square root (`√100`) · 3 a cube (`4³`) · 4 a cube root
(`∛27`) · 5 trapping `√40` between two whole numbers · 6 word problem (400 paving
stones). No item copies a check or workbook question; maths is in `$$…$$`.
