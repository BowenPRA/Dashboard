# U01_1 · 1.1 Adding & Subtracting Integers (self-study)

The independent-learning **rebuild** of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U01_1`). The old type-based U01_1 is
replaced against the layout system and the check gate (ADAPTATION-PLAN §8).

**Objective:** I can add and subtract positive and negative integers on a number
line, and turn the English of a question into the right calculation.
**Key words:** integer, positive, negative, inverse, difference, number line —
the one that costs marks is **difference** ("the difference between −4 and 7" is
a subtraction, and nothing on the page says so).

## The drill (NUM_DRILL, column-add-sub)
Operands only, `[a, b, op]`; `NumberDrill.jsx` derives every carry (for +) and
borrow (for −) from `utils/columnArithmetic.js`. Regrouping is the skill the
number line hides. Warm-up (no regroup) → Regroup (carries and borrows) →
Stretch (`268+47`, `402−158` across a zero).
