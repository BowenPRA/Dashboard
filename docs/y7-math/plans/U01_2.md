# U01_2 · 1.2 Multiplying & Dividing Integers (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U01_2`). Built as the template for
1.1 and 1.3–1.6 — see [../ADAPTATION-PLAN.md](../ADAPTATION-PLAN.md).

**Objective:** I can multiply and divide positive and negative integers, and I
know when "two negatives make a positive" is true (× and ÷) and when it is a lie
(addition).
**Key words:** product, multiply, divide, quotient, brackets, estimate — the one
that costs marks is **product** ("find the product" means ×).

## The deck (14 slides, ↓ from 21 classroom slides)
Hero+starter · what ×-negative means (REPEATED_JUMPS) · **hook** keep the pattern
going (PATTERN_LADDER) · neg×neg derived (PATTERN_LADDER_SOLVED) **[check 1]** ·
all four rules / ÷ shares them (SIGN_GRID) **[check 2]** · English: is the
sentence always true? · key word **Product** **[check 3]** · English: which
number gets cut up? **[check 4]** · Say It Then Write It (TranslateWidget) ·
brackets first (BRACKETS_FIRST) **[check 5]** · The Empty Account (coins) · The
Durian Run (durian) · recap checklist · closer + exit question.

The five checks are the classroom "on your whiteboard before anyone answers"
moments: the pattern's next line, the four sign rules, product-vs-sum, which
number is divided, brackets-first. Each distractor is the mistake the slide was
written to catch (e.g. `−12` for neg×neg, `4` for the bracket trap). Correct
answers are spread A/C/B/A/C — Notes does not shuffle options.

## The drill (NUM_DRILL, long-mult)
Operands only; `NumberDrill.jsx` derives every partial product, carry and column
sum (`utils/columnArithmetic.js`). Warm-up `23×12, 31×21, 42×13` → Carries
`47×26, 68×34, 59×47` → Stretch `236×47, 418×65`. A rung unlocks when the one
above is clear; a slow student is held on Warm-up and still banks XP.

## Assessment coverage (6 items, distractors = diagnoses)

bracket trap `24÷(-2+-1)` · 5 worked backwards `-6×?=54` · 6 word problem
(submarine, `8×-7`). No item copies a check or workbook question; maths is in
