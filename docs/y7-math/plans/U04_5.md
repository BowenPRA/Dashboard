# U04_5 · 4.5 Making decimal calculations easier (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U04_5`, 20 slides, one period), built to
the number-engines unit shape in [../number-engines.md](../number-engines.md) with its
own engine, Make It Easy ([../decimal-engines.md](../decimal-engines.md) §4.5).
**English and French only** (`meta.vietnamese: false`), like the deck.

**Objective:** I can write a decimal as an equivalent fraction; multiply by a decimal
by dividing by 10 or 100 first, and say that × a decimal less than 1 makes a number
smaller; use half of, a quarter of and a tenth of; work out × 9 and × 11 from × 10;
and partition a decimal to multiply it in parts — choosing the easy way first.
**Key words:** equivalent, equivalent fraction, partitioning, half of, a quarter of,
lots of — the section's two words, the word inside the first, and the three phrases
the questions are written in.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U04_5` ("Maths 4.5 · Making
Calculations Easier"). The classroom lesson's `meta.dashboard` back-link is not set
by this build (the classroom repo is read-only here).
