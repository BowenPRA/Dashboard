# U04_2 · 4.2 Adding and Subtracting Decimals (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the **4.2 part** of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U04_1_4`, which taught 4.1–4.4 in one
period): slides 6–9 (the vote on 1 − 0.72, the LineUp widget, the rule, mental and
written method), 18b (12 − 4.5 = 8.5), 19 (the English that picks the operation),
20 problem 1 (change from 10 dollars) and 21 (Mr Bowen vs Usain Bolt). The rest is
built from the book's 4.2. Engine and deck activity:
[../decimal-engines.md](../decimal-engines.md) §4.2.

**English and French only** (`meta.vietnamese: false`), as the classroom deck is.

**Objective:** I can add and subtract decimals in columns — line up the points, fill
the gaps with zeros, exchange when the top digit is too small, and bring the point
straight down; check a subtraction by adding; use a mental method when a number is
nearly whole; and read "change" and "how much more" as a subtraction.
**Key words:** decimal point, line up, exchange, mental method, written method,
change — the point the method hangs on, the two moves it is made of, the book's two
exam instructions, and the shop word that means subtract.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U04_1_4` (the same entry as the other
4.1–4.4 units). The lesson's `meta.dashboard` back-link is not set by this build.
