# U02_1 · 2.1 Constructing Expressions (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U02_1`), built to the template 1.2
established (ADAPTATION-PLAN §8). First unit of Unit 2, and the first algebra.

**Objective:** I can choose a letter to represent a number I cannot count,
leave c − 50 unfinished without thinking it is wrong, write 3 × s as 3s, and
read the English that decides the operation.
**Key words:** expression, represent, unknown, total, difference, subtract from —
the ones that cost marks are **represent** (Q10 marks on it) and **subtract
from** (one preposition, opposite answer).
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U02_1`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here. See
[classroom-dashboard-pairing.md](../../classroom-dashboard-pairing.md).

## The deck (14 slides, ↓ from 23 classroom slides)
Hero + starter (count the particles) · Count Them (PARTICLE_DROP hook) · Give
It a Letter (**represent**) **[check 1]** · The Row That Will Not Finish
(COFFEE_WALL — the wall, kept whole) · key word **Expression** **[check 2]** ·
The Short Way (NOTATION, reveal) **[check 3]** · The Words Do the Choosing
(FOUR_PHRASES, salt folded in) · Four Beakers (reveal) · Total and Difference
(TOTAL_DIFFERENCE, reveal) · The Words Come Backwards (ORDER_FLIP, the
"which is right" question folded in) **[check 4]** · Subtract 4 or Subtract
From 4 (SUBTRACT_VS_FROM, the compare folded in) · The Result (ORDER_OF_OPS)

The five checks: what n represents, which is an expression, the short way of
5 × t, "9 less than m", and "subtract the result from 30". Correct answers
spread A/C/A/B/B.
