# U02_3 · 2.3 Collecting Like Terms (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U02_3`), and the first unit built to
the Year 7 algebra shape ([../algebra-engines.md](../algebra-engines.md)): an
interactive deck, Collect It and Algebra Pyramids.

**Objective:** I can say what a term and like terms are, simplify an expression
by collecting like terms — counting the invisible 1 and moving every sign with
its term — and leave unlike terms such as 3a + 2b alone.
**Key words:** term, like terms, simplify, collecting like terms, simplest form,
expression — the workbook's key-words box plus the two the questions lean on.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U02_3`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here.

## Cut / changed from the classroom deck
- **Votes → predicts:** both "left hand A, right hand B" slides are `predict`
  activities on the vote slide (the hand-drawn ANS_* cards were dropped).
- **Whiteboards → typed:** Tick or Cross types e (a–d in a reveal); Keep the Sign
  types 9 + 5t − 4 − 2t (6p + 2q − 4p + 3q in a reveal); the brick rows keep a
  and b in a reveal and ask row c as a check.
- **Room game → sort:** Like or Not? (16 cards) is an 8-pair `sort`.
- **Mr Bowen's homework:** redrawn with six lines, two right, marked 6/6, and
  sorted right/wrong (four mistakes kept: no like terms, the invisible 1, unlike
  terms joined, ab = ba).
- **Folded:** the starter check slide into the starter predict; the pens word
  problem cut (a slide with an interaction keeps to the perimeter).
- **Word problems:** perimeter typed (`algebra`); the animals and the keys are
  checks, so the goldfish (+ x) and the keys (81k⁴) are named distractors.
- **Added:** an `estimate` on the real bricks (x = 22 cm, how long is 5x?).
- **Cut:** the date, the homework slide, the Lesson Complete exit hero (the exit
  question is the final check).

## Collect It ladder
One kind (4h + 5h, 6w + 2w + 3w, 9r − 4r) · the invisible 1 (k + 7k, 10e − e,
g + g + 5g) · two kinds (5a + 3b + 2a + 4b, 2p + 6 + 4p + 3, **3u + 8v — cannot
simplify**) · keep the sign (8x + 3y − 5x + y, 12 + 6n − 5 − 4n, 4c − 7d + c + 2d)
· tricky kinds (2mn + 5 + 3nm − 1, 3t² + 4t + t² − t, **6y² + 2y — cannot
simplify**) · lengths (rectangle 3a + 2 by a, equilateral triangle x + 2y,
ribbon 10p − 3p − 2).
