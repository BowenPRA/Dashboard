# U03_1 · 3.1 Multiplying and Dividing by Powers of 10 (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the **first half** of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U03_1_2`, slides 1–18), built to the
Unit 3 shape in [../number-engines.md](../number-engines.md) and the deck density
in [../algebra-engines.md](../algebra-engines.md) §4. The classroom teaches 3.1
and 3.2 as one deck over two periods; the Dashboard follows the book, so the
second half is [U03_2](U03_2.md).

**Objective:** I can say what a power is and read it out loud, know that the
power counts the zeros, multiply and divide by powers of 10 by moving the digits
left or right — not by "adding zeros" — and change between milligrams, grams,
kilograms and tonnes.
**Key words:** power, powers of 10, place value, digit, placeholder, decimal
point — the section's two margin words (power, powers of 10), the table the
method runs on (place value), what moves (digit), what does not (decimal point),
and the zero that holds an empty column open (placeholder).
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U03_1_2` (the same entry U03_2
carries). The classroom lesson's `meta.dashboard` still has to point back here —
it holds one `{ track, unit }`, and this deck has two units.

## Cut / changed from the classroom deck
- **Starter + "How Many Zeros?" → one `predict`** on slide 2 (10, 100, 1000 in
  the sub-line, the ten-zero number to count). Slide 3 settles it with 10¹⁰.
- **The folk rule and the hand vote → one `predict`** (slide 7), with a third
  option, 72000 ("take the point out, then add three zeros"). The ANS_* vote
  cards were dropped from diagrams.js. The stepper settles it on slide 8.
- **PlaceShift is two slides**: ShiftLeft (7.2 × 10³, 6.5 × 10⁴, and 0.38 × 10²,
  where no zero appears at all) and ShiftRight (48600 ÷ 10³, 702 ÷ 10⁴,
  35 ÷ 10²). A digit now slides to its new column (it is keyed by the column it
  started in) and a placeholder fades in once the column is clear.
- **Which Way? (room game) → a `sort`** of eight cards into left / right; the
  widget is not ported. Quick Fire deals the cards in the practice phase.
- **Whiteboard slides → activities**: one question slid or chosen, the rest in a
  reveal (slides 6, 9, 15, 17). Slide 12 keeps 7000 ÷ 10² as its check.
- **New slide 10, "Where the Zero Comes From"** (ADD_ZERO, new diagram): why the
  folk rule looked right — 56 × 10 leaves the ones column empty, 7.2 × 10 does
  not. The classroom note "adding zeros only works when there is no decimal
  point" was rewritten: a zero is written only when a column is left empty.
- **New slide 14, "Placeholder"** (PLACEHOLDERS, new diagram): the key word gets
  its own slide before the student divides; the classroom folded it into the
  dividing note.
- **The chain comes before the missing power** (classroom 14, 13 → 16, 17): it
  keeps two `shift` activities apart, and "5 places left, so × 10⁵" leads into
  "count the places — that is the power".
- **Mass**: the four units are put in order first (`order`), the classroom's
  reveal "how many mg in 4 kg?" is the `convert` activity, and a new check goes
  up the ladder (Mr Bowen's motorbike, 115 kg → 0.115 t).
- **Moon and Jupiter**: the Moon is the check, Jupiter stays in the reveal, and
  "the power told you which was further" became its own check (slide 22), with
  the condition the classroom left unsaid — the number in front is between 1
  and 10.
- **The place-value table closes the unit** with a `hotspot` on PLACE_COLUMNS
  (new: the table's columns with every text tagged `keep`, because a hotspot
  strips printed labels; headed Th H T O · t h th like the Slide the Digits
  table, since the full words are unreadable in the side column). One sentence
  points on to 3.2.
- **Cut:** the date, the starter card on the hero, "5 written panels", "talk to
  the person next to you", every whiteboard / hands instruction, the homework
  slide and the exit hero (the exit question is the checklist's check).
- **Icons:** `ListOrdered`, `ArrowLeft` and `Table` are not in the Dashboard's
  icon map, so slides 5, 9 and 23 use `Hash`, `MoveHorizontal` and `Columns3`.
