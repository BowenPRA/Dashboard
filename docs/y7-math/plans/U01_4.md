## The drill (NUM_DRILL, long-div, exact)
The bus-stop method (`NumberDrill.jsx` long-div view): the student fills the
quotient digit above the bar, then the product and the remainder, one step per
dividend digit; `utils/columnArithmetic.js` derives every value. "Does it divide
exactly?" is the factor test, so every dividend is a multiple of its divisor and
the final remainder is 0. Warm-up → two-digit divisors → four-digit dividends.
