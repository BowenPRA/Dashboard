## The drill (NUM_DRILL, short-div, exact)
The bus stop, written the short way — the same screen as 3.2's Bus Stop
(`ShortDivision.jsx`): the student types the quotient digit on top and each
remainder small, up and to the left of the next digit, and what is left at the
end after an r. No products or subtractions under the number (the long form was
replaced on 2026-10-09). `utils/shortDivision.js` derives every value. "Does it divide
exactly?" is the factor test, so every dividend is a multiple of its divisor and
the final remainder is 0. Warm-up → two-digit divisors → four-digit dividends.
