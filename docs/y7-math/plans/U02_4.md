# U02_4 · 2.4 Expanding Brackets (self-study)

The independent-learning adaptation of the classroom deck
(`C:\Users\bowen\lessons`, `content/y7-math/U02_4`), and the first Y7 unit built
to the algebra-engines shape and deck density
([../algebra-engines.md](../algebra-engines.md) §1, §4).

**Objective:** I can expand brackets by multiplying every term inside by the
number outside — taking the sign into the box with its term, multiplying the
numbers and keeping the letter — stop when the terms are not like terms, and
expand and simplify in that order.
**Key words:** brackets, expand, multiply out, term, like terms, simplify — the
workbook's two, the deck's third (the exercise switches to "multiply out"
without warning), and the three from 2.3 that "expand and simplify" leans on.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-math/U02_4`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here.

- **Votes → predicts.** Both hand votes are predicts on the vote slide; the
  answer cards (ANS_*) were redrawn with an A/B letter badge instead of a raised
  hand.
- **Whiteboards → typed.** Each whiteboard slide keeps its list with a reveal and
  gains one typed or grid item; the classroom's own numbers stay in the deck.
- **Right or Wrong? → sort.** The room game widget is dropped; 8 of its cards
