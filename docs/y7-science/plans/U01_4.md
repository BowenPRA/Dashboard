## Practice workbook — why these shapes
Sorting the ladder's own examples (dnd) and matching organ system → shared job
(dnd) rehearse the two things the deck's activities already trained; ordering
smallest-to-largest (order) is the same task as the deck's order activity with
different examples, for repetition without repetition; the dropdown sentences
(inline) rehearse the exact page-24 wording and the tissue countable/
uncountable distinction; fill_blank drills "epithelium" and "palisade/spongy"
by typing; the one typed question (4 leaf tissues + 4 digestive organs = 8)
keeps a real small calculation. No two consecutive questions share a type.
