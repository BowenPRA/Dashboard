## Practice workbook — why these shapes
`dnd` carries three different jobs — sorting (f3: heating vs cooling),
one-to-one matching (p2: change → from/to, p4: scenario → change) — because
that is what the exam and the classroom both actually ask for. `order`
rehearses the investigation method itself. `inline` (f2, c2) drills the
verb/noun pairing and the boiling-plateau reasoning in sentence form.
`fill_blank` (f4) is the two fixed points, 0 °C and 100 °C. The one typed
question (c1) is a real calculation — start temperature plus rate × time —
in the style of U01_1's scale-factor question. No two consecutive questions
share a type.
