# U02_7 · 2.7 Compounds and Mixtures (self-study)

Follows [particle-engines.md](../particle-engines.md) (the 2.5–2.7 shape) and
[ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md). Reduced from the classroom lesson
`y7-science/U02_7` (Learner's Book pp. 64–69).

**Objective:** tell a compound from a mixture and explain the difference, using
iron and sulfur stirred and then heated; give air, mineral water and tap water
as mixtures; say what pure means in science.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-science/U02_7`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here.

## What was cut or changed, and why
- **Hero:** date and the paper starter gone; the starter is slide 2's sort.
- **Three hand votes** → `predict`s; the ANS_* vote-card diagrams are dropped.
- **Draw This** (mixed then bonded) → the diagram stays, and the student tests
  boxes of those particles with a magnet instead of drawing them.
- **"What is air? Tell your partner"** → classify three boxes of gas.
- **Room game Mixture or Compound?** (the `MixtureOrCompound` widget) → not
  ported; its pairs became the particle activities on slides 22–23, and fish
  sauce survives in Workbook c1.
- **Homework** (copy a bottle's label) and the **"Lesson Complete" exit hero**
  → gone; the exit question is the checklist's final check.
- **"Look in your kettle at home"** → "the white crust inside an old kettle".
- **The 10 write panels** stay as orange Key word cards with the badge renamed
  (the default badge reads "Write This Down / Chép vào vở").
- **Iron is grey**, not the book's orange, in every diagram: the deck puts
  MIX_COMPOUND beside boxes drawn by `utils/particles.js`, whose iron is grey.
- **EMISSIONS** embedded two photos inside an SVG with Vite imports; slide 16 is
  a `compare` of the same two photos, so `diagrams.js` imports nothing and
  `svg-coords.mjs` can load it.
- **Added:** the formula activity, the four-box magnet test (spare iron / spare
  sulfur), the nitrogen estimate, and "a mixture's composition can change, a
  compound's cannot" on slide 15 (used by shortQA sq2).
- **Pipe-clay triangle** (a book key word) is never used in the section and is
  left out of `realWords`, as in the classroom plan.
