shape plus Water Journey) and [ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md). Reduced

with particles, and know that water vapour is an invisible gas.

**Why the `cycle` slides are short callouts.** From lg a `cycle` activity is a column
beside the slide (`SIDE_ACTIVITIES` in `src/tasks/Notes.jsx`, with `chain` and `ph`; at
1280×720 the diagram is 408×240, 324×190 for a journey, and a tap target ~44 px
across). On a phone it is a footer under the slide that takes up to half the card. So
each `cycle` activity has a slide of its own with one or two lines (8, 18, 23; slide
11's split is short too), and the teaching and Key word cards sit on the slide before.

## Water Journey (`src/utils/waterCycle.js`)
- **Places (6):** open water, the atmosphere, a cloud, the land, groundwater, a plant.
- **Processes (7), only the deck's arrows, the deck's names:** evaporation (open
  water → atmosphere; liquid → gas; heat in), transpiration (plant → atmosphere;
  liquid → gas; heat in), condensation (atmosphere → cloud; gas → liquid; heat out),
  precipitation (cloud → land *or* open water; no change of state — snow and hail froze
  inside the cloud, the fall changes nothing), surface run-off (land → open water),
  soaking in (land → groundwater), groundwater flow (groundwater → open water). Eight
  arrows (precipitation has two). No roots-take-up arrow: the deck never draws one.
- **`cycleSvg()`** 800 × 470, the classroom palette, cloud and trees; every arrow a
  cubic curve whose midpoint is its tap target, targets ≥ 113 apart (hit radius 44).
- **EVERYDAY: 26 sentences** (the twelve WhichStage cards and fourteen more), each with
  its process, the trap it invites and a one-line why.
- **Modes:** arrow (one lit, four options incl. the near-miss), tap (pick a ring, then
  Check), journey (3–4 processes; marked by walking the graph from A — any order that
  ends at B is right; the bank is never itself a right order), state (state change ×
  heat), everyday. Wrong answers are answered by name first.
- **Checker:** catalogue (graph ↔ drawing, target spacing, EVERYDAY ≥ 20) plus 60
  rounds per mode; every option, tap, state pair and journey permutation is proved to
  mark exactly as the graph says.
- **Screen:** a fixed-height round in which the diagram is the one thing that shrinks,
  so no round scrolls at 1280×720 or 375×812, in English or Vietnamese.

## What was cut or changed, and why
- **Hero:** date and the paper starter gone; the starter is slide 2's predict.
- **"Where has this water been?"** had no answer on its slide → folded into slide 3.
- **The hand vote** → a predict; the ANS_YES / ANS_NO vote cards are dropped.
- **PRECIP_KINDS** embedded three photos with Vite imports → slide 13 is a `compare`
  of the same photos, so `diagrams.js` imports nothing and `svg-coords.mjs` loads it.
- **WhichStage** (a presenter-clicker widget) is not ported: its cards are the slide-22
  sort and the task's `everyday` mode. No `widgets.jsx`.
- **Draw This** → the diagram stays (slide 5); students tap, name and order its arrows.
- **The poster homework, peer assessment and the "Lesson Complete" hero** → gone; the
  exit question is the checklist's check. Book Q5 and Q8 (research) are left out, as in
  the classroom plan.
- **The seven write panels** stay as Key word cards (badge renamed).
- **mist.jpg** was the classroom's evaporation photo; here it sits on the "Can you see
  water vapour?" slide, where mist being liquid is the point.
- **Steam** is never used: 2.2 defines it as a gas, the classroom 2.4 calls the visible
  cloud steam. The deck says "the white cloud above a kettle" instead.
- **Added:** "what the particles do" (slide 19 — heat in going up, heat out
  condensing, no change of state falling) for the task's `state` mode; groundwater
  flowing to the sea, which the classroom diagram draws but its slides never say.
