## Practice workbook — why these shapes
`dnd` sorts properties and matches state → particle arrangement (the two
things the exam actually asks); `order` rehearses "strongest to weakest
attractive force" and the compress-a-gas sequence; `inline` dropdowns
rehearse the "liquids and gases share pouring, only a gas compresses" share/
not-share distinction and the vacuum sentence; `fill_blank` drills the exact
words "50 cm³ / different", "hypothesis / theory"; one plain typed question
keeps the "volume stays 50 cm³ however you pour it" fact as a number, not a
choice; the Challenge `dnd` (limitations of a marbles-in-a-box gas model)
extends the sponge slide's strengths/weaknesses idea. No two consecutive
questions share a type — six distinct types appear across 12 questions
(mcq · inline · dnd · fill_blank · order · typed).

## Label It
Three diagrams, 6/6/5 pins, one or two distractors each. Pins sit at a
label's leader-line end or, where a caption has no leader line, its own text
position (`node scripts/svg-coords.mjs Y7_SCI/U02_1 <KEY>` for `diagrams.js`
keys, `... <KEY> diagramsB` for the particle diagrams). Bank text is authored
fresh rather than copied from the SVG caption, since the engine strips every
printed `<text>` at runtime:
- **Syringe test** (`DIAGRAMS.SYRINGES`) — water/air syringe, plunger, thumb
  over the hole, moves/does not move. Distractors: *compressed*, *the
  container*.
- **Three arrangements** (`PARTICLES.THREE_ARRANGEMENTS`) — solid/liquid/gas
  names plus their short arrangement description. Distractor: *vibrating on
  the spot only* (true of a solid, placed to catch a student sorting by
  keyword instead of position).
- **Why the gas compresses** (`PARTICLES.COMPRESSING_GAS`) — big gaps/small
  gaps, push, "nothing escapes", "only the gaps shrink". Distractor: *more
  particles added* (the misconception the slide explicitly rules out).
