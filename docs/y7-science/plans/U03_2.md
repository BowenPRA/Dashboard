# U03_2 · 3.2 Formation of the Solar System (self-study)

> Built 29 Oct 2026 as planned and approved (quiz gate 65 approved; no `scale` kind).
> The changes made while building are under **As built** at the end.

Two classroom decks become ONE self-study deck (pairing rule 10; precedent
`U02_model_states` → `Y7_SCI/U02_2`): `y7-science/U03_2` (27 slides, Learner's Book
pp. 85–90) and `y7-science/U03_models` ("Models: Space", 20 slides). `meta.classroom`
lists both, in teaching order (slugs `U03_2`, `U03_models`). **English and French only**
(`meta.vietnamese: false`). Not absorbed: `U03_coffee`, `U03_earth`, `U03_lab`.

**Objective:** stars and planets form from clouds of dust and gas (nebulas); gravity
pulls the dust together, and more mass gives stronger gravity, so a clump pulls in even
more; the story in order with the cause of each step (it shrinks → spins faster and
flattens → the middle gets hot enough → the Sun → the disc clumps into planets → rocky
near the Sun, giants far out); the facts used as evidence, and the two that seem to
contradict the hypothesis; what a model is for, and its limitations.

**The thread:** "Nobody was there — so how do we know?" One question holds the whole
deck together: the words (evidence, hypothesis, support, contradict), the facts, the
models, and the hypothesis are all parts of the answer.

## Storyboard (`SOLAR_STORY`, p84): `utils/solarStory.js`, `tasks/Storyboard.jsx`
**Why an engine:** the book's own Activity (p. 89) is a storyboard: nebula → how the Sun forms
→ how planets form, *with the reasons why*. No existing task marks a reason for each step.
- **Tables:** `STEPS`, 7 frames named as in Formation (Cloud, Collapse, Disc, Sun, Clumps,
  Planets, Today), each with a drawn `frameSvg` in the NEBULA_STEPS style; `CAUSES`, the cause of
  each step, worded as the deck's panels word it; `TRAPS`, each `{ id, en, fr, why, whyFr,
  fits }` (the Sun's heat pulled the dust / magnetism / it spun slower / spinning made a ball /
  something set the middle on fire / every clump becomes a star / gravity grows with time / small
  clumps pull harder / heavy rock sank to the Sun / the Sun's gravity sets the direction / just
  luck); `FACTS` (`verdict` supports | contradicts and the `frame` it supports: same direction,
  one plane, the Sun's spin, moons, HL Tauri, Orion, rocky/giant, 99.8% of mass, Venus, Uranus);
  `MODELS` (dust, drawing, football, formation), with statements tagged strength | limitation.
- **Item:** `{ id, level, kind: 'story', from, to, ask? }` · `{ id, level, kind: 'evidence',
  fact }` · `{ id, level, kind: 'model', model }`.
- **Stages, all derived:** *story* — Order (the window's frames, a seeded derangement) →
  Because (a cause for each asked link, from a bank of the right causes plus 2 traps that fit the
  window). *evidence* — Supports or seems to contradict? → which frame (supports) or "what do
  scientists do?" (contradicts: throw it away / keep it and look for an explanation ✓ / change
  the fact). *model* — sort 4 statements → strength / limitation.
- **Named slips:** `order` (the first pair out of order, with the cause between them), `trap:*`
  (11, each with its own why), `elsewhere` (a true cause on the wrong link), `verdict`,
  `proves` (contradicts read as "the hypothesis is wrong"), `frame`, `mixed` (a statement in the
  wrong bin).
- **12 items, 6 levels:** 2 short stories · 3 evidence · 1 four-frame story · 2 models · the
  whole story (7 frames, 3 causes asked) · 99.8%, Venus, Uranus.
- **Proof:** `checkStoryConfig` (validator marker) runs every item's `slipCases`: the canonical
  answer is accepted, each slip is fired by its canonical wrong answer, no trap is a right cause
  in its window, every table entry has its French twin. In Node: every window × link subset,
  with 0 slip collisions.
- A second wrong answer (or Show me) fills the stage in, and the item pays half. Blob `{ id: 1 | 0.5 }`.
