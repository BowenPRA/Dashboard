# U03_1 · 3.1 Gravity, Weight and Mass (self-study)

The U02_8 shape ([unit2-close-engines.md](../unit2-close-engines.md) §1) with its own
engine, **Weigh It** (`WEIGH_IT`, p80). Reduced from the classroom lesson
`y7-science/U03_1` — one deck taught over two periods (45 slides) — to ONE deck
(pairing rule 10). The first Year 7 Science unit in **English and French only**
(`meta.vietnamese: false`): `…Fr` twins everywhere, no `…Vn`.

**Objective:** gravity pulls objects towards each other and acts towards the centre of
the Earth; weight (N) and the contact force that balances it; mass (kg), which does not
change; W = m × 10 and on other planets; falling things accelerate, and heavy and light
things land together.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-science/U03_1`. The lesson's `meta.dashboard`
back-link is added at integration (it is in the other repo).

## Weigh It (`utils/weighIt.js`, `tasks/WeighIt.jsx`)
**Why a new engine.** LAB_BENCH reads instruments; REARRANGE ("Isolate It") changes the
subject algebraically with SI conversion — beyond Year 7 and EN/VN only. The book teaches
the **formula triangle** with g from a planet table, so Weigh It is that, derived.
- **Item:** `{ id, level, kind: 'weight' | 'mass' | 'same' | 'two', m | W, place, from?,
  context?, contextFr? }`; places are the deck's PLANET_TABLE.
- **Stages:** Cover (tap W or m on the triangle) → Set up (× or ÷, and g from a planet
  strip) → Calculate (number + N/kg toggle; "750 N" typed works). `same`: Decide (× g,
  ÷ g, stays the same) → Calculate. `two`: the stages twice — the mass, then the weight.
- **Slips named:** cover, cover-g, op, earthg, wrongg, unit (kg written as N),
  nochange, point (estimate given to 1 s.f.), changed (mass changed on the Moon).
- **Proof:** `checkWeighConfig` (validator marker) runs every item's `slipCases` —
  its own answer accepted, each slip fired by its canonical wrong answer — and refuses
  non-terminating division, more than 2 d.p., a context without its number. In Node:
  189 cases over the 15 items, and 4000 random items with 0 slip collisions.
- Second wrong (or Show me) fills the stage in; the item pays half. Blob `{ id: 1 | 0.5 }`.
