Follows the U01_1 exemplar for [ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md).

**Objective:** name the parts of an animal cell, say how it differs from a
plant cell, and learn the method for looking at your own cells.

## Cut, and why
- The paper starter ("list plant parts starting with **c**, on paper") and
  its follow-up "check your list" slide (organelle vs substance) — the
  starter is gone, so the framing no longer applies. That check question is
  the one dropped of the original five (the other four survive as three
  checks plus the new interactive activities).
- "In pairs", "two minutes, no calling out", "write it down before the next
  slide" on the old Spot the Difference slide — replaced by the hotspot
  activity itself, which scores the tap directly.
- "Copy this drawing and label all four parts" (`drawThis` on the animal-cell
  slide) — dropped entirely; Label It is now where the student places every
  label, so a second "copy it by hand" instruction was redundant. U01_1 has
  no `drawThis` slide either, so this keeps the exemplar's pattern.
- "Your turn — finish the sentence **on paper**, then check" → "…then check"
  (same reveal, no paper).
- "Answer in a full sentence **on paper**" on the Plant or Animal? widget
  slide → dropped; the widget itself is the interaction.
- "**Draw** one or two of the cells you can see, and label your drawing" —
  dropped from the microscope steps; drawing a live specimen doesn't map to
  a screen task, and the order activity tests the same procedural knowledge.
- The recap's "Your notebook should now have **7 things written down** and
  **1 labelled drawing**. Check." — removed; replaced with a forward pointer
  to Label It and Practice.
- The classroom's group build-a-cell activity and the homework slide were
  already absent from the prior self-study reduction; nothing further to cut
  there.
- Kept: the `tone: 'write'` definition panels and the "You Will Need" /
  "Safety" boxes on the practical slide — these are in-app styling, not
  instructions to use paper, and match the exemplar's usage.

## Interactive activities
- **predict** (`a1`, slide 2) — "How many cells are in one person?", four
  orders of magnitude as options, correct = 100 trillion. The `explain` pays
  it off with the galaxy comparison; the next slide (unchanged from before)
  elaborates with the Milky Way photo.
- **hotspot** (`a2`, slide 5) — `DIAGRAMS.SPOT_THE_DIFFERENCE`, target the sap
  vacuole. Coordinates read via `node scripts/svg-coords.mjs Y7_SCI/U01_2
  SPOT_THE_DIFFERENCE`; decoys are deliberately parts *every* cell has
  (nucleus and a mitochondrion, on both sides of the diagram) rather than the
  other two plant-only parts, so a student who taps the wall or a chloroplast
  by mistake isn't being tricked by a genuinely-correct-elsewhere answer.
- **order** (`a3`, slide 15) — the seven steps end to end: rub cheek, rub
  slide, add methylene blue, lower cover slip, smallest objective lens, look
  from the side, look down the eyepiece. Sits after both `steps`-layout
  teaching slides, so every step it tests was taught first.

## Practice workbook — why these shapes (11 Q)
Sorting parts into "every cell" / "plant only" (dnd) and matching part → job
(dnd) are the two things the exam asks; the coarse-grained stage order
(order: make the slide → set up the microscope → focus → look) rehearses the
procedure at a different grain than the deck's fine-grained order activity;
the two dropdown sentences (inline) rehearse "similar vs the same" and the
reliable-clue wording; the typed question re-asks "how many cells, in
trillions" from the deck's predict activity; the two fill_blank questions
complete "no cell wall → no fixed shape" and the stain/objective
definitions; the Challenge dnd (true reasons Lan might be wrong about the
onion) extends the reliable-clue idea to a specific false-reasoning case. No
two consecutive questions share a type.

## Label It (2 diagrams)
- **animal** — `DIAGRAMS.ANIMAL_CELL`, 4 pins at each label's leader-line end
  (`node scripts/svg-coords.mjs Y7_SCI/U01_2 ANIMAL_CELL`); bank carries one
  distractor (*cell wall*, plant-only).
- **slide_prep** — `DIAGRAMS.SLIDE_PREP`, 4 pins, one per numbered box, using
  the hand/tool endpoint in each box as the pin (there is no organelle-style
  leader line here, since the diagram illustrates a procedure, not a
  labelled structure); bank carries two distractors (*look down the
  eyepiece*, *choose the smallest objective lens* — real steps from the
  deck, but not pictured on this diagram). `SPOT_THE_DIFFERENCE` was
  considered for the second diagram but has no per-part labels at all (by
  design, for the hotspot activity), so it doesn't fit Label It's "tap the
  labelled part" shape.

## What could not be done
Nothing from the brief was dropped outright. The one judgement call: the
plan's per-unit table suggests `order` for "making the slide" as the
mid-deck activity, which is what slide 15 does — but it tests all seven
steps (both slide-prep and microscope-setup) rather than only the four
slide-prep steps, since the shorter list didn't leave enough for a
meaningfully-shuffled order task on its own.
