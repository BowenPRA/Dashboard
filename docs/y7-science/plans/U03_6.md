The self-study twin of the classroom deck `y7-science/U03_6` (20 slides, one period,
Learner's Book pp. 111–116). **English and French only** (`meta.vietnamese: false`).
The deck's three short commands stay as the thread: **change it** (a bouncing ball:
one press, one change; an event or a process), **draw it** (energy-change diagrams:
a box for each kind of energy, an arrow for each change, chains and two arrows from
one box) and **follow it** (useful and dangerous changes, the candle's heat, heat moves
from hotter to colder). 3.5's stores, transfers and the eight energy names are assumed
knowledge, used but not taught again. 3.7's work (useful versus *wasted* energy, and
energy spreading out) is left to 3.7: here the ball's and the candle's "missing" energy
is named (sound and heat; the glass, the tripod and the air) and no further.

**Objective:** I can say that when something happens — an event or a process — energy
is changed or transferred; draw an energy-change diagram with a box for each kind of
energy and an arrow for each change, including a chain (a gas power station) and one
box with two arrows (a television); give a useful energy change and a dangerous one;
and say which way thermal energy moves: from hotter things to colder things.

'Science 3.6 · Changes in Energy' }]`. The back-link is the lead's.

## The engine: From… To… (`ENERGY_CHANGE`, p90), unit key `energyChange`
**Why an engine:** the section's skill *is* the energy-change diagram (the book's three,
Questions 2–3, the power station, the exit), and no task marks one. SEQUENCE marks order
without the boxes; LABEL_IT names parts of a fixed drawing. The classroom's own game is
already the right shape — a picture, then *from*, then *to* — so the engine takes its
name, its pictures and its eight energies.

Files: `utils/energyChange.js` (model, stages, diagnosis), `utils/energyChangeData.js`
(the eight energies; the scenes, each `{ id, en, fr, happens: event|process, diagram,
traps }`), `utils/energyChangeCheck.js` (the proof), `tasks/EnergyChange.jsx` +
`components/science/energychange/` (the board, the palette), deck activity
`energychange` (`components/notes/EnergyChangeActivity.jsx`, in Notes' `SIDE_ACTIVITIES`).
The pictures are the unit's (its `art.js`, passed in data.js), like 3.2's frames.

A diagram is a **set of arrows** between the eight names (chemical, kinetic,
gravitational potential, elastic potential, thermal, electrical, sound, light). The
student's answer is compared as a set, so the order of the boxes after a branch never
matters, and every slip below is computed from the two sets.

```js
energyChange: {
  title, titleFr, levels: { 1: { en, fr }, … },
  items: [
    { id: 'k1', level: 1, kind: 'one', scene: 'kettle' },           // walked: event or process? → from → to
    { id: 'r1', level: 3, kind: 'read', diagram: ['gravitational', 'kinetic'] }, // which picture shows it?
    { id: 'b1', level: 4, kind: 'branch', scene: 'tv' },             // shape → start → the outputs
    { id: 'c1', level: 5, kind: 'chain', scene: 'powerStation' },    // shape → box by box (each box has its picture)
    { id: 'h1', level: 6, kind: 'heat', hot: { en: 'your hand', t: 35 }, cold: { en: 'an ice cube', t: 0 } },
  ],                                                                 // + walk: true | false
}
```

**Stages (walked).** *one*: Event or process? → From (tap one of the eight) → To.
*branch* / *chain*: Shape (a row of boxes, or one box with arrows to the others; the box
count is given) → Start → the other boxes. *read*: tap the picture the diagram shows (of
three). *heat*: Which is hotter? → Which way does thermal energy move? (three arrows,
one of them "cold moves in") → What happens to each? (the hotter one cools, the colder
one warms). Derived from the two temperatures.

**The fading rule** (as Maths 5.1). Levels 1–2 walk every stage; from level 3 an item
opens **quick**: fill the boxes (and pick the shape) and press Check once. A wrong answer
names its slip and opens the stage it belongs to; the first item of a new kind walks.

**Named slips:** `backwards` (from and to swapped — the bow, the falling apple, and
charging, where the why is the battery: "a battery is a chemical store"); `fromWrong`,
`toWrong`, `same`; `notTheJob` (a true side effect, not what it is for: "a kettle does
make a little sound, but we use it to heat water"); `trap:<id>` (each scene's own
misconception with its why: the solar panel's *thermal*, the candle's *thermal* when it
lights a room); `chained` (a TV drawn electrical → sound → light), `branched` (the power
station drawn as one box with three arrows), `order` (two links swapped: the generator
before the gas burns), `missing` (one output left out); `event`/`process` (with the
definition); heat: `coldMoves`, `wrongWay`, `then`. Each is named and tried again; a
second wrong (or Show me) fills the stage in and the item pays ½. Blob `{ id: 1 | 0.5 }`.

**Levels (16 items):**
1. One change, walked — a kettle (process; electrical → thermal), a falling apple (event;
   gravitational potential → kinetic).
2. One change, walked, with traps — a candle lights a dark room (chemical → light), a bow
   shoots an arrow (event; elastic potential → kinetic), a phone charging (electrical →
   chemical).
3. Quick — a solar panel (light → electrical), a drum (kinetic → sound), **read**:
   gravitational potential → kinetic — a falling book / a bird flying higher / a bow.
4. Two arrows from one box — a television (walked: electrical → sound, light), a hair
   dryer (quick: electrical → thermal, kinetic).
5. Chains — the gas power station (walked: chemical → thermal → kinetic → electrical), a
   hydroelectric dam (gravitational potential → kinetic → electrical), a wind turbine
   lighting a lamp (kinetic → electrical → light).
6. Heat and mixed — an ice cube in your hand (walked), hot soup in a cool kitchen, a
   phone in use (a chain, then two arrows: chemical → electrical → light, sound).

**Proof** (`checkEnergyChangeConfig`, at the `@@SCI_U03_6_CHECK@@` marker; the deck's
three builds too): every stage accepts its own answer; every named slip fires on its
canonical wrong answer and on nothing else; no trap equals the answer or another slip;
every name is one of the eight; a `read` item's distractors are scenes whose diagram is
not the asked one; a heat item has two different temperatures; every scene and message
has its French. A Node sweep over every scene × every one-box change as well.

**Touch and phones.** Tap an energy then a box (or the box first), or drag; tap a filled
box to empty it. The board is HTML, not one wide SVG: at 375 px a chain stacks downwards
and a branch's outputs wrap under their box. The palette shows the eight as words with
3.5's action figures (the same stick figures the class used standing up).
