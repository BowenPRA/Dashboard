# U02_6 · 2.6 Compounds and Formulae (self-study)

Follows [particle-engines.md](../particle-engines.md) (the 2.5–2.7 shape) and
[ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md). Reduced from the classroom lesson
`y7-science/U02_6` (28 slides, Learner's Book pp. 57–63) to the density of the
Extended/Additional Maths decks: the student does something every slide or two.

**Objective:** explain the difference between an element and a compound; name
compounds with -ide and -ate and the prefixes mono and di; read a formula to
say which elements and how many atoms.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-science/U02_6`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here.

## What was cut or changed, and why
- **Hero starter** "test your partner on the symbols" → the symbol sort on slide
  2 (the six symbols the lesson uses, with the C/Ca trap).
- **Both hand votes** (slides 3 and 17) → `predict` activities. The dangers
  slide and its vote are one slide (the photo with the predict beside it); the
  vote cards `ANS_*` are dropped from the diagrams.
- **Questions 1–2** get their own check on the New Properties slide (the
  misconception that a compound keeps its elements' properties) with the
  model answers in a reveal, instead of a separate question slide.
- **Element or Compound?** (the room game) → two `particles` activities: `kind`
  on slide 18 and `find` on the Question 10 slide. O₃ and S₈ are not drawable;
  O₂, N₂ and Cl₂ carry the "two atoms, one kind" trap instead, and Mg stands in
  for Question 10's single-atom metals (K, Al, Ca are not drawable).
- **Draw This: four particles** → the diagram stays as the answer to the O₂
  predict; drawing becomes the formula-write activity and Particle Lab's
  `formula`/`build` rounds. Label It names the four particles too.
- **Question slides 10–17** → one scored item each (Q10 `particles`, Q13 check,
  Q17 check) with every other question and answer kept in the reveal. Q11
  (SO₂) moved into the Q10 reveal.
- **Homework** (paper models of five compounds) and the **"Lesson Complete!"
  exit hero** → cut; the exit question (CaCO₃) is the scored `formula` count on
  the checklist, the deck's last item.
- **Write-note panels** stay, relabelled "Remember this / Ghi nhớ" instead of
  "Write This Down / Chép vào vở" (no notebook instructions).
- **Checklist** loses "check your notebook: 8 panels + 1 drawing"; adds new
  properties and reading a particle diagram.
- The **ElementOrCompound** widget is not ported (hand votes, clicker keys).
  **NameCompound** and **FormulaReader** port unchanged apart from dropping the
  unused imports.

## Label It
No diagram has leader lines, so every pin sits on its label's text position
(`node scripts/svg-coords.mjs Y7_SCI/U02_6 <KEY>`). Stripping also removes the
atom letters, so the PARTICLES and PREFIXES titles give the colour key.
Distractors: carbon monoxide and hydrogen (particles); "two atoms of the same
kind" (element/compound); carbon oxide and "tri = three oxygens" (prefixes).
`FORMULA_READ` is not used: its text is the whole diagram.
