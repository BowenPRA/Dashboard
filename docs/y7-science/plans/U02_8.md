# U02_8 · 2.8 Acids and Bases (self-study)

Follows [unit2-close-engines.md](../unit2-close-engines.md) §1 and §4 (the 2.3 / 2.4 /
2.8 shape) and [ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md). Reduced from the classroom
lesson `y7-science/U02_8`. There are no Learner's Book pages behind 2.8 — Unit 2 of
the book ends at 2.7 — so, like the deck, the unit follows the Cambridge Lower
Secondary Stage 7 content and the classroom's own whole-number pH values.

**Objective:** name acids and bases met every day; test a liquid with litmus and
universal indicator; read the pH scale and call a liquid acid, neutral or alkali;
explain what neutralisation does and where it is used.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-science/U02_8`. The classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` back-link is added at integration (it is in the other repo).

## What was cut or changed, and why
- **Hero:** date and the paper starter gone; the starter is slide 2's sort.
- **The vote** (pH 1 or pH 13) and the two ask-first slides ("So what is left?",
  "Mr Bowen ate too much") → `predict`s answered on the next slide; the ANS_A /
  ANS_B vote cards are dropped from `diagrams.js`.
- **The two glasses** are closed by the student: slide 12's `ph` litmus activity
  names only "soapy water", so the student must know it is an alkali.
- **Draw This** (the scale; acid + alkali) → the diagrams stay without the badge;
  the second carries the drops activity.
- **PhDipper** ports as-is (colours now from `utils/phLab.js`, "Say it" → "Decide").
  **Acid Snap** (a whole-room clicker game) does not port: its cards became the
  `order` activity on slide 26 and the pH Lab catalogue.
- **Questions 1–6** → Q5 and Q6 checks; Q1–4 in a reveal; Q1 again as the colour
  activity. **Homework** (hunt your kitchen) → one optional line on the checklist.
  **Exit hero** → the checklist's check.
- **The seven write panels** stay as Key word cards (slide 14 merges two into one
  card so the split fits a 1280×720 laptop).
- **`ph` activities ride on showcase slides.** Notes keeps `ph` out of its
  side-panel list, so it sits in the footer under the slide (capped at 55%); a
  showcase picture shrinks to make room where a statement's big text would scroll.
  That is why slides 12 and 17 are photo showcases and slide 25 exists.
- **Added:** the sour sort, the corrosive spill check, the hotspot, and the four
  `ph` activities. Nothing in the science is new: every pH value is the deck's
  (PhDipper, Acid Snap) or a school-chart standard.

## pH Lab (`utils/phLab.js`, `tasks/PhLab.jsx`)
- `SCALE`: the classroom PH_SCALE colours, pH 1–14, with colour words; `kindOf`;
  `strengthOf` says "strong" only at 1–2 and 12–14 and "weak" only at 4–6 and 8–10
  (3 and 11 are drawn differently on different charts); no round asks for a band.
- `SUBSTANCES`: 26, bilingual — the deck's PhDipper and Acid Snap values plus
  tomato juice 4, sugar water 7, milk of magnesia 10, ammonia cleaner 11, drain
  cleaner 14. Left out on purpose: shampoo, green tea, bleach, rain.
- Modes: **classify** · **colour** (pH → four swatches at least 3 apart, or a
  tube → its pH) · **litmus** (blue and red, pH or kind shown) · **strength**
  (strongest acid / alkali, sometimes with the other family as the trap; or order
  3–4, most acidic first) · **neutralise** (drops from pH 1–5 or 9–13 until green;
  one past green ends the round; or a NEUTRAL_LIFE / Q6 case: what to add and
  which way the pH goes).
- Deck activity `ph`: `ask: colour | place | litmus | drops`, `substance` or
  `substances` (place, 1–4), `pH`, `within: 1` (place), `show: name | pH | kind`.
- The validator draws 60 rounds per mode and proves each round accepts its own
  answer and rejects a wrong one; `checkPhActivity` does the same per activity.
