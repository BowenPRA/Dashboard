## Practice workbook — why these shapes
Two dropdown sentence frames (inline) rehearse the exact "adapted to ___
because it has ___" wording for two different cells; the animal/plant sort
(dnd) and the harder cell→adaptation match (dnd) are the two things the exam
actually asks; ordering the path water takes into a root hair cell (order)
rehearses a sequence, not a fact; the typed "how many metres" keeps one real
number; two fill_blank questions drill vocabulary (haemoglobin, cilia,
specialised) the audio narrates but the workbook never tests elsewhere. No
two consecutive questions share a type.
