# U02_5 · 2.5 Atoms, Elements and the Periodic Table (self-study)

Follows [particle-engines.md](../particle-engines.md) and
[ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md). Reduced from the classroom lesson
`y7-science/U02_5` (one lesson → one unit). The densest science deck so far:
the student taps, guesses, predicts or sorts on 18 of 26 slides, and the new
`periodic` activity and Element Hunt task make the Periodic Table something
they *use* rather than look at.

**Objective:** explain what an atom and an element are, describe how atoms join
together, find periods, groups, metals and non-metals in the first 20 elements,
write element symbols with the capital-letter rule.
**Classroom twin:** `meta.classroom` → `y7-science/U02_5`; the classroom lesson's
`meta.dashboard` points back here.

## What was cut or changed, and why
- **Hero starter** "draw the particles in a solid" → the slide-2 hotspot on a
  new drawn `PARTICLE_BOXES` (liquid, gas, solid). A hotspot rather than a
  sort: sort cards are words, and the point is to recognise the *picture*. The
  boxes' only text is "Box 1–3", so stripping it costs nothing.
- **Date, "Mr Bowen"** gone (the halving widget's cube is "a 1 cm gold cube").
- **Question-first slides**: "Can you cut forever?" → predict; "How many cuts?"
  → estimate; "Why Na?" → predict, each answered on the next slide.
- **"How many elements?"** (its own Think slide) folded into the Element slide
  as its check, so the deck does not have two unscored slides in a row.
- **Draw This** (how atoms join) → the drawing stays as a reference slide; the
  check on the photo slide after it asks for it from memory; Label It has the
  student place all four captions; Workbook p2 sorts six elements by it.
- **"Book page 54: put your finger on a period"** → `TableExplore` on the write
  slide, then the group-1 `periodic` tap.
- **Metal quiz vote + reveal** → a check on the photo slide (bromine); the
  Metals write slide carries the period-3-metals tap instead of the reveal.
- **Symbol Snap** (room game) → the `sort` of symbol-making ways, the
  names-hidden potassium tap, and the Element Hunt task (which does what the
  game did, with marking and fresh rounds).
- **Book questions** (four slides, two questions each) → two slides with four
  each; the hardest of each set (Q4 potassium, Q8 the helium group) is a
  `periodic` tap, the rest are in the reveal. Q2 and Q6 are also deck checks.
  `TableExplore`/`TableMass` no longer sit beside the questions — the activity
  draws its own table.
- **"Lesson Complete" exit hero** → the exit question is the checklist's check.
  "Check your notebook: 8 key words and 1 drawing" is gone.
- The TWO_MEANINGS and SYMBOL_WAYS diagrams keep their printed text, so neither
  is used for a hotspot or Label It.

## Practice workbook — why these shapes
Six widgets, no two in a row alike. Typed boxes take **numbers only** (f4: 94
and 118; c1: 10 mm halved three times = 1.25 mm): the workbook marks typed
answers algebraically, which would read "Na" as N × a. Typed symbols with the
capital rule are Element Hunt's `symbol` mode, which diagnoses them properly.
`dnd` sorts metals (f2) and how atoms join (p2) and matches symbols to names
(p4, K and Na among them); `order` reads the table for mass (p1); `inline`
drills period/group (f3) and graphite/diamond (c2); `mcq` covers element (f1),
same period as magnesium (p3, the book's Q7 turned round) and "CL" (c3).

`JOINING` (no leader lines: pins at each panel's caption; distractor "atoms of
two different elements joined"), `PT_MAP` (new: coloured first-20 tiles with
group 1 and period 3 outlined and five leader lines; pins at the line ends;
distractor "Helium: the lightest atoms"), `SIZE_LADDER` (new: cube, sand, hair,
cell, virus, atom; pins at the captions, whose cut counts are stripped with
them; distractor "a grain of rice"). Coordinates from
`node scripts/svg-coords.mjs Y7_SCI/U02_5 <KEY>`. The photo panels live in
`diagramsB.js` because they import `assetUrl`, which does not load in plain
Node; the drawn diagrams stay in `diagrams.js` so the coordinate script works.
