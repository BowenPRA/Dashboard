# U01_1 · 1.1 Cells (self-study, engagement rebuild)

The exemplar for [ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md](../ENGAGEMENT-PLAN.md): the first
science unit rebuilt so the student *does* things rather than reads them. The
other five units follow this shape.

**Objective:** define a cell and its organelles, compare animal and plant
cells, explain how a microscope magnifies.
**Classroom twin:** `y7-science/U01_1`.

## The deck (16 slides)
1 hero ("in this lesson" card — no paper task) · **2 estimate**: how tall would
Mr Bowen be if a cell were a soda can (slider, km) · 3 the working (ScaleChallenge
widget) · 4 taller than Everest · 5 scale ladder · **6 check**: what is a cell (+
the *cella* reveal) · 7 prison cell / Hooke's cork · 8 inside the tiny room
(CellExplorer widget) · **9 hotspot**: tap the nucleus on the unlabelled animal
cell · **10 check**: which organelle releases energy · 11 plant-only parts 1 ·
**12 check**: why chloroplasts are green · **13 sort**: seven parts into "every
cell" / "plant cells only" · 14 the same two cells, for real · 15 how a
microscope magnifies (+ the electron microscope) · 16 models and the recap.

Cut from the first self-study version: "write a sentence on paper" starter,
"copy this diagram into your notebook", "in class you will build models with
Mr Seth" (kept as one line), the notebook-count line, the separate electron
microscope slide (folded into 15).

## Practice workbook — why these shapes
Sorting parts (dnd) and matching part → job (dnd) are the two things the exam
asks; ordering sizes (order) rehearses the scale ladder; the dropdown sentences
(inline) rehearse the exact wording of the definitions; the typed 120 ÷ 0.02
keeps one real calculation. No two consecutive questions share a type.

## Label It
Pins sit where each label's leader line ended in the classroom diagram
(`node scripts/svg-coords.mjs Y7_SCI/U01_1 <KEY>`). Distractors: *cell wall* on
the animal cell, *cellulose* on the plant cell, *focusing knob* on the microscope.
