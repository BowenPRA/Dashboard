# Year 7 Cambridge Science — the course spine

The self-study track `Y7_SCI` (Cambridge Lower Secondary Science 7, Learner's
Book), the twin of the classroom `y7-science` course. Read
[classroom-dashboard-pairing.md](classroom-dashboard-pairing.md) first — every
unit here is a reduction of a deck taught in the room, and that doc is the
contract for how the reduction is made.

## 1. The unit = one Learner's Book section

Same rule as Maths ([y7-math-course.md](y7-math-course.md)): one Cambridge
section is one app unit, zero-padded so it sorts in book order (`U01_1` …
`U02_2`). Where the classroom taught a section as two lessons (`U02_1a` +
`U02_1b`), the Dashboard unit absorbs both and lists both in
`meta.classroom`.

**Bilingual EN + VN, always.** Same students as Maths, and the science words
("organelle", "compress", "condensation") *are* half the lesson.

## 2. The unit shape

Seven scored tasks. **130 XP is available and the unit caps at 100**, so a
student can drop a whole task and still finish. Two gates, both under the 80%
rule ([phase-gate-80-percent-rule]).

| Order | Phase | Gate | Task | XP | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `concept` | 0 | `NOTES` — the deck | 20 | 16–22 slides, **5 checks** |
| 2 | `concept` | 0 | `WORD_REC` — Vocab | 20 | 8–9 key words, audio |
| 3 | `practice` | 30 | `SPELLING` | 10 | the same words, typed |
| 4 | `practice` | 30 | `READ_COMP` — Reading | 20 | **3 cloze passages** |
| 5 | `practice` | 30 | `SHORT_ANSWERS` — Questions | 20 | **4 reasoning Qs**, AI-marked |
| 6 | `practice` | 30 | `DIAGRAMS` — label and explain | 20 | **3 items** on the unit's own SVGs, AI-marked |
| 7 | `mastery` | 80 | `ASSESSMENT` — Quiz | 20 | **6 MCQ**, 8 minutes |
| — | `mastery` | 80 | `GAMES` — arcade | 0 | level from `unitDifficulty.js` |

Why this differs from the Maths shape: no Workbook (there is no exercise of
answers to reveal), no Number Gym; instead the three tasks an ESL science
student actually needs — spell the words, read the register of the book, and
write an explanation that is marked on its science *and* its English.
`DIAGRAMS` sits in **practice**, not mastery, so the quiz gate is 80 of the 110
XP before it (73%); with it in mastery the gate would be 89% and the validator
refuses it.

## 3. The per-file recipe

```
src/data/Y7_SCI/<UNIT>/
  data.js        meta (id, title, desc, track, icon, classroom), phases,
                 realWords, passages, shortQA, diagrams, + the imports below
  notes.js       the deck — layout slides, 5 checks, `check:` last on its slide
  diagrams.js    copied verbatim from the classroom lesson (diagramsB.js for a
                 second absorbed lesson)
  widgets.jsx    copied from the classroom lesson, photo imports rewritten to
                 assetUrl(...)
  assessment.js  { timeLimit: 480, passages: [], questions: [6 × mcq] }
  games.js       arcadeConfig('Y7_SCI', '<UNIT>')
public/images/Y7_SCI/<UNIT>/   the licensed photographs (never lb-* scans)
public/audio/Y7_SCI/<UNIT>/    generated by `npm run sync-audio`
```

### Vocabulary (`realWords`)
8–9 words. Take the copy-down definitions from the deck's `write` notes, then
add the one or two words the *questions* lean on that the book never defines
("similar", "specimen", "trillion"). `def` is one plain sentence; `sent` uses
the word in the unit's own context. No `fakeWords` needed.

### Reading (`passages`)
Three passages of 80–110 words each, in the register of the Learner's Book,
covering the deck in order. Blanks are `{word}` and must be words from
`realWords` (the task shows the first letters as a hint, so a blank that is
not a taught word is a guess). Two or three blanks per passage.

### Questions (`shortQA`)
Four, two marks each, one mark per `markScheme` line. They are the deck's
ask-first questions and the book's page questions, rewritten so a student
can answer from the deck: "explain why", "compare", "what is the clue". The
`vnTranslation` is the whole question in Vietnamese. See
[question-quality.md](question-quality.md) for suggested words and the
grading model.

### Diagrams (`diagrams`)
Three items, each on one of the unit's own SVGs (`DIAGRAMS.X`), 2–3 marks:
name the parts, state the function, explain the mechanism. Pick the diagrams
that carry the *idea* (the three-arrangements picture, the heating curve, the
five-levels ladder), not the decorative ones.

### Quiz (`assessment`)
Six MCQ, four options, correct letters spread so no letter is never right
(the validator warns). 1–2 key words in context, 3–5 the core idea, 6 a
transfer question the deck did not answer directly (the jellyfish, the sperm
cell). Every explanation says why each distractor is wrong.

## 5. Verify

`preview-y7sci.html?unit=<UNIT>` mounts all seven tasks without login
(`http://localhost:5173/Dashboard/preview-y7sci.html?unit=U02_1`). Then
`npm run validate`, `npm run audit:svg Y7_SCI`, `npm run lint`. The arcade
level for each unit is in `src/components/towerdefense/unitDifficulty.js`
(`TRACK_LEVELS.Y7_SCI`) — add a row when a unit is added, or it plays flat.
