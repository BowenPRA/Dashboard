# C5.01 Exothermic and endothermic reactions — lesson & unit plan (COORD_SCI / U05_1)

The second unit of the **IGCSE Coordinated Science** track (`COORD_SCI`), built
from the Cambridge coursebook section in
`docs/coord-science/sources/C5.01_Exothermic_Endothermic_Reactions.pdf`
(pages 345–349, figures C5.03–C5.11, questions C5.01–C5.06).
This track is **English-only** (`bilingual: false` in the track registry), so
there are no `vn*` twins.

## Objective

The student can tell an exothermic reaction from an endothermic one by what
happens to the **surroundings**, say what "the surroundings" includes and why the
thermometer is part of them, **draw and read an energy level diagram** (axes,
level lines, the ΔH arrow, the activation-energy hump), give ΔH its **sign** and
units, explain any reaction through **bonds broken vs bonds made**, and say why
every reaction has an activation energy however exothermic it is.

## The new task — Energy Diagrams (`ENERGY_PROFILE`)

The coursebook's Worked Example C5.01 is a seven-step recipe for **drawing** a
reaction pathway diagram, so the student draws one. For each reaction they:

1. move the **products line** above or below the fixed reactants line;
2. drag out the **activation-energy hump**, which must clear **both** lines;
3. label the **enthalpy change** ΔH negative or positive.

The diagram redraws live under their hands, so the shape and the chemistry stay
welded together. Every judgement is **derived from the geometry** by
`src/utils/energyProfile.js` — nothing is compared against a stored picture — and
`checkItem` rejects any item whose stated ΔH sign contradicts its stated type, so
a wrong key cannot ship. Component: `src/tasks/EnergyProfile.jsx`;
registry entry `ENERGY_PROFILE`, dbKey `p23`, nativeMax 10, default 20 XP.

**Why the y-axis carries no numbers.** The book's own figures (C5.05, C5.06,
C5.10) have an unlabelled `Energy / kJ` axis: the diagram is qualitative and the
mark scheme awards the *shape*, not a height. So the task grades the three things
an examiner actually ticks, and prints the item's real ΔH on the arrow once the
diagram is built — the number is taught without being faked into a pixel height.

The endothermic items are where the third check earns its keep: a hump that does
not clear the products line is not a reaction pathway, so a student cannot get
there by copying the exothermic shape.

Items: methane (−728) · nitrogen monoxide (+180) · magnesium (−1204) ·
photosynthesis (+2803) · zinc + copper(II) sulfate (−217, question C5.02) ·
calcium carbonate (+178 — the "hot kiln so it must be exothermic" trap).

## Equations (`SYMBOL_EQ`) carries forward

Step 2 of the Worked Example is "work out the formulae and write a balanced
symbol equation" — the labels on the two energy levels **are** the equation, so
the task from U04_1 stays. Items: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO · CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O ·
N₂ + O₂ → 2NO · 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O · 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂ (question C5.05) ·
Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu. U04_1's Formulae slot is dropped; this unit's new skill
is the diagram.

## Diagrams (authored, no third-party imagery)

`diagrams.js` holds six, drawn to match the coursebook figures: `HOT_COLD_TUBES`
(C5.04), `ENERGY_EXO` (C5.05/C5.07), `ENERGY_ENDO` (C5.06), `ACTIVATION_PAIR`
(C5.10), `MEXOBENDO` (C5.09) and `BOND_ROLLERCOASTER` (C5.08/C5.11).

These are **authored, not downloaded**. A reaction pathway diagram is line art —
axes, two level lines, one arrow — so drawing it here beats sourcing a bitmap: it
stays crisp at any size, matches the deck's palette instead of fighting it, the
labels are real `<text>` the SVG audit can measure, and there is no third-party
licence to carry (so no `CREDITS.json`, as in U04_1).

Colour is used consistently across the whole unit: **warm red = energy out**,
**cool blue = energy in**, **key orange = activation energy**, green = the
coursebook's energy level lines.

## Two fixes this unit forced

- **`scripts/svg-audit.mjs` only matched `KEY: \`<svg…\`` blocks**, so the decks
  that build each diagram as `const KEY = \`<svg…\`` first (this unit, U04_1 and
  ADD_MATH/AM_3A) reported "0 findings" because nothing was ever read. The regex
  now accepts both styles. This unit is clean; the newly-visible findings in
  U04_1 are pre-existing and listed there, not fixed here.
- **The notes icon resolver is a whitelist** that silently renders `Info` for an
  unlisted name. The chemistry/energetics icons this deck needs were added — and
  so were `Grid3x3`, `Move` and `ArrowLeftRight`, which U04_1 had been asking for
  and quietly not getting.
